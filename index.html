Bring buyers together around a property or portfolio offered as is. MREO’s auction-style connection gives each interested buyer an opportunity to improve an offer, helping you pursue top dollar without making repairs first.

$1Participation starts with one dollar for buyers and sellers.
$1,000 / propertySuccess fee, due only when a sale closes. For portfolios, the fee is $1,000 for each property sold.
21 daysStandard auction period. This test version starts with one day.

With clear pricing instead of an open-ended assignment-fee model, you can see how much of an offer remains after MREO’s fee. Set the amount you want to receive; the required bid adds MREO’s $1,000-per-property success fee. Any additional bid value remains in your sale proceeds.

Competitive bidding is designed to improve price discovery; the final price depends on buyer interest and the condition of the property or portfolio. Other closing costs and obligations are separate.
